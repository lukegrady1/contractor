## FAQ
1. **How long does a typical kitchen remodel take?** - A standard full-gut renovation typically takes between 8 and 10 weeks once construction begins. This includes demolition, electrical/plumbing rough-ins, cabinetry installation, and countertop fabrication. Custom orders for specialized materials may extend this timeline.
2. **What is the average cost of a high-end remodel?** - Costs vary significantly based on materials and scope. Most our projects range from $50,000 to $120,000+. We provide a detailed line-item estimate after our initial design consultation to ensure there are no surprises.
3. **Do you handle all necessary permits?** - Yes. Image Contracting handles the entire permitting process from start to finish. We work directly with local building departments in Bedford, NH and surrounding towns to ensure all structural, electrical, and plumbing work is fully compliant and inspected.
4. **Can I live in my home during the renovation?** - Most clients do remain in their homes. We use advanced HEPA air filtration and plastic dust barriers to minimize impact on your living space. However, keep in mind that your kitchen will be non-functional for several weeks; we recommend setting up a temporary galley in another room.
