The Image Standard

Founded in Bedford, NH, Image Contracting began with a single truck and a simple philosophy: if you do it right the first time, your reputation will do the selling for you. Today, we manage multi-million dollar remodeling projects and commercial developments, but that core philosophy remains unchanged.

We believe in "clean-site" protocols and radical transparency. Every client receives a detailed project timeline and a dedicated site manager. When we say we'll be there at 7:00 AM, our boots are on the ground at 6:55 AM.
